How to Install AI Keyboard

Set up in 30 seconds

Tapix installs as a native iOS keyboard extension. Enable it once, use it in every app on your iPhone — forever.

01

Download & enable

Get Tapix free from the App Store. Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Tapix.

02

Switch to Tapix

Tap the globe icon on your keyboard to switch to Tapix — in iMessage, WhatsApp, Mail, Instagram, any app.

03

Tap an AI action

Type normally, then tap Fix It, Translate, Tone, or Shorten. Text transforms inline — instantly ready to send.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Tapix.

What is Tapix? +

Tapix is a free AI-powered keyboard extension for iPhone. It fixes grammar and spelling, translates text into 57 languages, adjusts writing tone, shortens messages, and suggests smart replies — all inline, without leaving the app you're typing in. It works in iMessage, WhatsApp, Instagram, Mail, Slack, and every app on your iPhone.

How do I install Tapix on my iPhone? +

Download from the App Store, then go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and select Tapix. Switch using the globe icon.

Is Tapix free to use? +

Yes. 15 free AI actions every day, no account required. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited at $4.99/month or $29.99/year (save 50%).

Does Tapix work in WhatsApp, Instagram, and other apps? +

Yes. Tapix is a native iOS keyboard extension — it works in every app where you can type. iMessage, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Twitter/X, Mail, Slack, LinkedIn, Notes, and more.

Is my data safe with Tapix? +

Absolutely. Tapix is built with privacy at its core. There is no keystroke logging — text is sent to AI providers (Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini) only when you explicitly tap an action, processed in real-time, and immediately discarded. No ad tracking, no data storage. Anonymized usage analytics contain no text content. Your text is never saved, logged, or used for AI model training.

What languages does Tapix translate? +

Tapix supports 57 languages including Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Italian, Hindi, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Greek, Czech, Romanian, Hungarian, and more. Just select a target language and translate inline with one tap.

Which AI model powers Tapix? +

Tapix uses advanced AI language models — Anthropic Claude as the primary provider and Google Gemini as a fallback — to process text. When you tap an AI action, your text is sent securely to the AI provider, processed in real-time, and the result is returned to your keyboard. The text is never stored, logged, or used for model training. No data is sent unless you explicitly trigger an action.

Does Tapix work offline? +

Tapix requires an internet connection to process AI actions, since text is sent to AI servers for processing. You can still use Tapix as a regular keyboard offline — only the AI features (Fix It, Translate, Tone, Shorten, Smart Reply) need connectivity.

Does Tapix support iPad? +

Tapix is currently designed and optimized for iPhone. iPad support is not available yet but is being explored for a future update. Stay tuned!

How accurate are the translations and grammar fixes? +

Tapix uses state-of-the-art AI models that deliver high-quality results for everyday messaging. Grammar corrections and translations work well for conversational text, emails, and social media. However, AI suggestions may not always be perfect — especially for highly technical, legal, or medical content. We recommend always reviewing the suggestion before sending.
